---
title: "Charges grouping"
description: "Assign prices (charges) to a group when adding or editing a charge."
---

Create a group with **Entity type** **Price** first ([Creating a group](/docs/product-catalogue/groups/create)). Then assign charges to that group using the steps below. In the dashboard, groups are called **tags**.

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan">
    - Go to **Product catalog** → **Plans** and open the plan
    - In the **Charges** card, click **Add charge**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the charge with a tag">
    - Choose **Fixed** or **Usage**, click **Continue**, and fill in the charge details
    - In **Tag**, search or select a tag (e.g. "GPU Usage"). The field appears once a tag that holds prices exists
    - Click **Create charge**; the price is stored with that tag
    <Frame>
      ![Add charge dialog with a tag chosen](/images/docs/groups/charge-tag.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The dashboard sets a price's tag only when the charge is added: editing a charge doesn't show the **Tag** field. To change or clear the tag later, update the price through the API (below).
</Note>

## API (prices)

| Action    | Method | Endpoint            |
|-----------|--------|---------------------|
| Create    | POST   | `/v1/prices`       |
| Update    | PUT    | `/v1/prices/:id`   |
| Get price | GET    | `/v1/prices/:id`   |
| Search    | POST   | `/v1/prices/search` |

**Create:** Include `group_id` (group `id` from `POST /v1/groups`).

**Update:** Send `group_id: "grp_..."` to assign, `group_id: ""` to ungroup. Omit to leave unchanged.

**View:** `GET /v1/prices/:id?expand=groups` or `POST /v1/prices/search` with `expand: "groups"` for full `group` on each price.

**Analytics:** `POST /v1/events/analytics` with `expand: ["price"]`. Group appears under each item's `price.group` when set.

**Invoices:** Line items use the price's `group_id` to fetch group names.

<Info>
  **Plan cloning** preserves groups. Cloned plan's prices keep the same group assignments as the source.
</Info>
