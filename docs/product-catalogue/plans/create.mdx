---
title: "Creating a plan"
description: "Follow the steps below to create a pricing plan in Flexprice:"
---

In the main navigation menu, open **Product catalog** → **Plans** and click **New plan** at the top right.

<Frame>
  ![Plans list with the New plan button](/images/docs/product-catalogue/plans/creating-a-plan/add-plan-button.png)
</Frame>

This opens a **four-step form**: **Basic info**, **Charges**, **Entitlements**, and **Credit grants**. Only the basic info is required; everything else can also be added later from the plan's page.

**Step 1: Enter plan details**

This step captures key identifiers for your pricing plan.

* **Name**: What the plan is called to customers and on invoices.

* **Lookup key** *(Optional)*: A stable identifier for the plan in API calls and system integrations. Use lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores.

* **Description** *(Optional)*: Internal context about the plan, shown alongside it in the dashboard.

* **Metadata** *(Optional)*: Your own key/value fields kept with the plan.

  <Frame>
    ![Plan details with a name, lookup key, and description](/images/docs/product-catalogue/plans/creating-a-plan/plan-details.png)
  </Frame>

Click **Next**.

**Step 2: Define plan charges**

It defines how customers will be charged under this plan. Click **Add charge** and choose **Fixed** or **Usage**. You can add **one or multiple** charges; each one you add is listed in the **Charges** card.

<Frame>
  ![Charges step listing a fixed platform fee and a usage charge for API calls](/images/docs/product-catalogue/plans/creating-a-plan/plan-charges.png)
</Frame>

* **Setting up a fixed charge:**
  Fixed charges are amounts your customers are billed at regular intervals (e.g., $49/month platform fee).

  <Frame>
    ![Fixed charge form with a display name, currency, billing period, invoice timing, free trial, and amount](/images/docs/product-catalogue/plans/creating-a-plan/recurring-charge.png)
  </Frame>

  * **Display name** *(Optional)*: What the charge is called on an invoice.

  * **Currency**
    Choose the currency in which you want to bill your customers. **Flexprice supports all currencies**, enabling businesses to charge customers globally.

  * **Billing period**
    The **billing period** determines when invoices are generated. The **billing period impacts invoice generation, feature limits, and renewals**. You can select from the following intervals:

    * **Daily** → Customers are billed every day.

    * **Weekly** → Customers are billed once every week.

    * **Monthly** → The most common cycle, where customers are charged every month.

    * **Quarterly** → Charges occur every 3 months.

    * **Half-yearly** → Customers are billed twice a year (every 6 months).

    * **Annual** → Customers are billed once per year.

    * **One-time** → The charge is billed once.

  * **Price unit** *(Optional)*: Price the charge in credits, tokens, or another custom unit instead of a currency.

  * **Invoice timing**: Decide when the charge is invoiced within the billing period:

    * **Advance** → Customers are charged **at the start** of each billing period.

    * **Arrears** → Customers are charged **at the end** of each billing period.

  * **Free trial (days)** *(Optional)*: Enter a trial duration. Subscribers are not billed for this charge during the trial; after the trial ends, the first charge is applied based on the **invoice timing** selected.

  * **Billing model** and **Amount**: Choose **Flat fee**, **Package**, or **Tiered**, and define the **amount** customers will be charged per billing period in the selected currency.

  * **Minimum quantity** *(Optional)*: The fewest units a subscriber can hold of this charge.

  * Click **Add charge** to add the fixed charge to the plan.

* **Setting up a usage-based charge:**
  To incorporate usage-based charges into a plan, you can utilize existing metered features. For instance, you can create charges based on the number of API calls, the number of active users, transactions, compute time, etc.

  <Frame>
    ![Usage charge form with the API calls metered feature, currency, billing period, and a flat fee amount](/images/docs/product-catalogue/plans/creating-a-plan/usage-based-charge.png)
  </Frame>

  * **Metered feature**
    Choose a metered feature that will determine how usage is measured and billed. Features must be pre-configured in your product catalog
    💡 *Only metered features can be linked to usage-based charges.*

  * **Display name** *(Optional)*: What the charge is called on an invoice.

  * **Currency**
    Select the currency in which you want to bill your customers. **Flexprice supports all currencies**, enabling businesses to charge customers globally.

  * **Billing period**
    The **billing period** determines how frequently usage is calculated and charged, from **Daily** to **Annual**. 💡 *The billing period must align with the subscription plan to ensure accurate invoicing.*

  * **Billing model**
    Flexprice allows different pricing models for usage-based billing:💡 *The correct billing model ensures flexibility in pricing strategy and revenue optimization. To read more about different types of charges read in the [Billing Models](/docs/product-catalogue/plans/billing-models/flat-fee) section.*

    * **Flat fee** → A fixed price per unit of usage (e.g., $0.01 per API call).

    * **Package** → Customers pay for usage in predefined bundles (e.g., $50 for 100K API calls).

    * **Tiered** → The price changes across usage bands (e.g., \$0.01 per API call for the first 100K calls, then \$0.008 for additional calls).

  * **Amount**
    Define how much you want to charge. The unit is determined by the **metered feature selected** (e.g., per API call, per GB stored, per transaction).

  * **Billing timing**
    Usage charges are billed in **arrears**, at the end of the period once its usage is known. 💡 *Arrears billing is common for metered services like cloud computing and APIs*

  * Once all configurations are set, click **Add charge** to add the usage-based charge to the plan.

Click **Next**.

**Step 3 and Step 4: Entitlements and credit grants** *(Optional)*

Add the features the plan includes in **Entitlements** (see [Link features to plans](/docs/product-catalogue/features/linking-to-plans)) and any credits it hands out in **Credit grants**, or skip both and add them later from the plan's page.

Click **Create plan** to finalize the pricing plan.
