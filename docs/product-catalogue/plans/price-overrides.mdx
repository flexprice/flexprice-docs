**Price Overrides** in Flexprice allow you to **customize pricing for specific customers** by modifying individual charges within a plan during subscription creation. This feature is particularly useful for offering special pricing, discounts, or custom rates for enterprise customers, early adopters, or promotional campaigns.

## When to Use Price Overrides

Price overrides are ideal for scenarios such as:

- **Enterprise deals** with custom pricing arrangements
- **Promotional pricing** for specific customers or campaigns
- **Volume discounts** applied at the customer level
- **Trial or beta pricing** for early adopters
- **Negotiated rates** for strategic partnerships

## How to Override Plan Prices

In the dashboard, subscriptions are called **contracts**, and you override prices while creating one.

### Step 1: Start a New Contract

- Go to **Customers** and open the customer
- Click **Create contract** in the **Contracts** card

### Step 2: Select a Plan

- In the **Contract** step, choose the pricing plan and click **Next**

### Step 3: Access the Charges Table

- The **Charges & add-ons** step shows a **table of charges** with every recurring and usage-based charge from the selected plan
- Each charge row displays the original pricing from the plan

### Step 4: Override Individual Charges

- Click the **⋯** menu on any charge row
- Select **Override price** from the dropdown menu

<Frame>
  ![Charge menu with Override price](/images/docs/product-catalogue/plans/price-overrides/override_dropdown.png)
</Frame>

### Step 5: Set the New Price

- A dialog titled **Override price** opens for the charge, with the **Plan price** from the plan shown at the top (read-only)
- Select the **Billing model** for the new price: **Flat fee**, **Package**, or **Tiered**. A new override starts on **Flat fee**, even when the plan charge uses another model.
- Fill in the fields for the selected model:
  - **Flat fee**: Enter the new price in **Amount**. On a usage charge, this is the price per unit.
  - **Package**: Enter **Price per package** and **Units per package**. Flexprice bills whole packages and rounds the quantity up to the next package.
  - **Tiered**: Pick a **Tier mode**. **Volume** bills the whole quantity at the rate of the band it falls in, and **Slab** bills each band at its own rate. Then fill in the **Tiers** table with one row per band: **Up to** (the highest quantity in the band), **Per unit**, and an optional **Flat fee**. The last band has no upper limit, and **Add tier** adds a band before it.

<Frame>
  ![Override price dialog with Flat fee selected and a new amount](/images/docs/product-catalogue/plans/price-overrides/price_override_dropdown.png)
</Frame>

- Click **Save override** to save the change

### Step 6: Review and Save

- The charges table shows the **overridden price**, with the plan price struck through and a **Price changed** flag
- You can override multiple charges within the same contract
- Click **Next** through the remaining steps, review the contract, and click **Create contract**

<Frame>
  ![Charges table with an overridden price and the Price changed flag](/images/docs/product-catalogue/plans/price-overrides/overriden_prices_summary.png)
</Frame>

## Important Notes

- **Plan Integrity**: Price overrides only affect the specific subscription being created. The original plan pricing remains unchanged for other customers.

- **Billing Consistency**: Overridden prices will be used for all future billing cycles unless the subscription is modified.

- **Audit Trail**: All price overrides are tracked and can be reviewed in the subscription history.

- **Currency Support**: Price overrides work with all supported currencies in your Flexprice account.

## Best Practices

- **Document Overrides**: Keep records of why specific overrides were applied for business continuity.

- **Regular Reviews**: Periodically review overridden subscriptions to ensure pricing remains appropriate.

- **Clear Communication**: Ensure your team understands when and how to use price overrides.

- **Validation**: Double-check override amounts before saving to avoid billing errors.

Price overrides provide the flexibility needed for complex pricing scenarios while maintaining the structure and consistency of your pricing plans.
