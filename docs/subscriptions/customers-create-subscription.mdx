title: "Create subscription for a customer"
description: "Once a customer is created, you can create a subscription for them by following these steps:"
---

In the dashboard, subscriptions are called **contracts**. You create one from the customer's page with the **New contract** flow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer">
    Go to **Customers** and click the customer. On the customer's page, click **Create contract** in the **Contracts** card.

    <Frame>
      ![Customer page with the Create contract button in the Contracts card](/images/docs/subscriptions/customers-create-subscription/customer-create-contract.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the plan and dates">
    In the **Contract** step, select a **Plan**, then check the **Billing period** and **Currency**. Set when the contract **Starts**. Set **Ends** only if the contract should stop on a date; by default it runs until cancelled. **Bill on**, **Free trial**, and **Proration** are optional. Click **Next**.

    <Frame>
      ![Contract step with a plan, billing period, currency, and start date](/images/docs/subscriptions/customers-create-subscription/contract-plan-and-dates.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the charges">
    The **Charges & add-ons** step lists every charge from the plan with its price. To change a charge for this customer only, open the charge's **⋯** menu and choose **Override price** or **Apply coupon**. See [Subscription Override Line Items](/docs/subscriptions/override-line-items) for what an override can change.

    Optionally, apply a **Coupon** to the whole contract or attach **Add-ons**. See [Add Addons to a Subscription](/docs/product-catalogue/addons/add-to-subscription). Click **Next**.

    <Frame>
      ![Charges and add-ons step listing the plan's charge with its price](/images/docs/subscriptions/customers-create-subscription/contract-charges.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set commitments and billing options">
    The **Commitments & benefits** and **Billing** steps are optional. They hold a contract commitment, credit grants, a tax rate, payment terms, and [customer hierarchy](/docs/subscriptions/customer-hierarchy). Click **Next** on each to keep the defaults.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the contract">
    On the **Review** step, check the summary and click **Create contract**. Flexprice returns you to the customer's page, where the contract appears in the **Contracts** card with its plan, status, and billing period.

    <Frame>
      ![Review step summarising the contract before it is created](/images/docs/subscriptions/customers-create-subscription/contract-review.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### **Managing Multiple Subscriptions for a Customer**

- A single customer can have multiple active subscriptions.

- Each subscription operates independently based on its assigned billing period, charges, and start date.

- For example, a customer can have both a `Basic Plan` and a `Premium Support` subscription, each billed separately. To bill optional extras on the same subscription and invoice instead, attach an [addon](/docs/product-catalogue/addons/overview).

### **Customizing Subscription Pricing**

- **Override Line Items**: Customize pricing for specific subscriptions by overriding plan prices
- **Special Pricing**: Offer enterprise discounts, bulk pricing, or promotional rates
- **Flexible Billing**: Modify billing models, tier structures, and quantities per subscription

For comprehensive information on customizing subscription pricing, see [Subscription Override Line Items](/docs/subscriptions/override-line-items).
