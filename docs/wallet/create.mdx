---
title: "Creating a wallet"
description: "Create a prepaid credit wallet for a customer from the Flexprice dashboard, choosing its name and currency, with one active wallet allowed per currency"
---

A wallet in Flexprice acts as a virtual account linked to a customer. It allows for storing and managing credits that can be applied toward subscription payments, usage-based charges, or other transactions.

Flexprice enable you to manage prepaid credits for your customers. These are credits added to a wallet and can be consumed for billing purposes. Wallets ensure an efficient and transparent mechanism for credit handling, aligning with subscription and usage-based pricing models. Wallets operate in a defined currency and can be topped up manually or automatically.

## Creating a wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer's Wallet tab">
    Go to **Customers**, open the customer, and select the **Wallet** tab. A customer without a wallet shows **No wallet yet**. Click **Create wallet**.

    <Frame>
      ![Wallet tab showing No wallet yet and the Create wallet button](/images/docs/wallet/creating-a-wallet/no-wallet-linked.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the wallet details">
    - **Wallet type**: **Prepaid** (credits are bought up front and usage draws them down in real time) or **Postpaid** (credits are applied to invoices at the end of the billing cycle).
    - **Currency**: the currency the wallet balance is valued in (for example USD or INR).
    - **Conversion rate**: how much money one credit is worth. The default is 1 credit = 1 unit of the currency.
    - **Top-up rate** (optional): what one credit costs when it's bought, if that differs from the conversion rate.
    - **Initial credits** (optional): free credits added as soon as the wallet is created. Set **Initial credits expire on** if they should lapse on a date.
    - **Description** (optional): a note about what the wallet is for. It labels the wallet on the **Wallet** tab.

    <Frame>
      ![Create wallet dialog with Prepaid selected, USD, a 1:1 conversion rate, and 500 initial credits](/images/docs/wallet/creating-a-wallet/wallet-form.png)
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      A customer can have only one active wallet per currency and wallet type. To replace one, select **Terminate wallet** in its **⋯** menu first.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the wallet">
    Click **Create wallet**. The **Wallet** tab shows the new wallet with its real-time balance, last invoiced balance and status, and its transactions, including any initial credits.

    <Frame>
      ![New wallet with its balances, status badges, and the initial credits transaction](/images/docs/wallet/creating-a-wallet/wallet-created.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add another wallet for the same customer, for example in a second currency, select **Create wallet** in the **⋯** menu on the **Wallet** tab.
