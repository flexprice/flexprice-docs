Flexprice connects to the tools you already use to collect payments, keep your books, and sell through cloud marketplaces. Each integration is built and maintained by Flexprice. Pick a category below to see what each connection syncs and how to set it up.

## Payments & Invoicing integrations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stripe" icon="/images/integrations/logos/stripe.svg" href="/integrations/stripe/connection-setup">
    Collect card payments, generate payment links, and sync customers and invoices with Stripe.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Razorpay" icon="/images/integrations/logos/razorpay.svg" href="/integrations/razorpay/connection-setup">
    Generate Razorpay payment links for invoices and sync customers between Flexprice and Razorpay.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Paddle" icon="/images/integrations/logos/paddle.svg" href="/integrations/paddle/connection-setup">
    Sync customers and invoices to Paddle and collect payments through the Paddle-hosted Flexprice Checkout.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Nomod" icon="/images/integrations/logos/nomod.svg" href="/integrations/nomod/connection-setup">
    Push invoices to Nomod, generate payment links, and receive payment events back through Zapier.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Moyasar" icon="/images/integrations/logos/moyasar.svg" href="/integrations/moyasar/connection-setup">
    Sync invoices to Moyasar, generate payment links, and enable autopay through the Flexprice checkout page.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Whop" icon="/images/integrations/logos/whop.svg" href="/integrations/whop/connection-setup">
    Push finalized invoices to Whop, get a Whop-hosted payment URL for each, and reconcile payments automatically.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Chargebee" icon="/images/integrations/logos/chargebee.svg" href="/integrations/chargebee/connection-setup">
    Sync items, customers, and invoices to Chargebee, and let Chargebee collect payment for each invoice.
